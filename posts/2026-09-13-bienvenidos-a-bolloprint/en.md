Almost every tool is born from a problem of its own. Ours was born when we
realised how much time we were wasting digging through sticky notes full of
orders, uploading every single listing to Etsy by hand, searching the supplier's
website for the temperature to print the filament we bought two months ago, or
chasing down which folder held the .stl with the final design
(`BolloBox_final_v2_thisONE_final.stl`).

Sounds familiar, right?

That's why we created BolloPrint. A management app designed specifically for 3D
printing workshops and print farms. We've built it piece by piece to solve the
specific pains of anyone running 2, 5 or 30 printers at the same time. We're not
a generic management tool shoehorned into the job. It's made by makers, for
makers.

## What will you find in BolloPrint?

Every tool you need so success doesn't bury you when the workshop starts asking
for more hours than the day has:

![Workshop dashboard with active orders, the status of each order, what's printing and recent activity](/blog/media/2026-09-13-bienvenidos-a-bolloprint/02-panel-pedidos.png)

- **Real cost calculation:** you'll know what each piece costs you, counting the
  filament, print hours, electricity… If you don't account for every cost,
  invoicing doesn't always mean making money.
- **Design library:** no more hunting for the final .stl. You open the file
  straight from the order, without diving through folders.
- **Order Kanban:** one glance tells you what's queued, what's printing and
  what's running late, without having to ask around.
- **Quotes:** no more spending hours on a quote that sometimes doesn't even get
  accepted… It got accepted? Turn it into an order with a single click.
- **Online store integration:** connect with Etsy (and soon Shopify) so you stop
  spending hours entering data by hand.
- **Printer control:** connect to your machines, whatever the brand.
- **Global materials catalogue:** more than 3,800 filaments and 750 resins (and
  counting) with their technical specs. You can also jot down your own printing
  tricks so you remember them next time.
- **Inventory control:** you won't overbuy materials when you know exactly what's
  in stock (filaments, resins, magnets, keyrings, isopropyl alcohol…).

![Global filament catalogue filtered by manufacturer, with the colour, material type, spec and SKU of each spool](/blog/media/2026-09-13-bienvenidos-a-bolloprint/03-catalogo-filamentos.png)

## So what's this blog for?

We're not going to fill it with boring press releases or filler articles. If you
come here, it's to take something useful back to the workshop:

1. **Tutorials and feature bites:** we'll explain parts of the app and tricks to
   get the most out of it day to day.
2. **Transparent development:** we'll tell you what we're working on and what's
   launching next (like invoicing integrated with Verifactu, Spain's e-invoicing
   system).
3. **Practical guides:** management and profitability tips to work out margins,
   organise orders and structure your workshop without losing your mind.
4. **Real cases:** things that work in other workshops and that you can apply to
   yours.

:::warning A note on how we work
We don't want to design behind closed doors. We want BolloPrint to be democratic
software, built hand in hand with the workshops that use it every day. If you
have an idea or need a specific feature, tell us and we'll look into it.
:::
