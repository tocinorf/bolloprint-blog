If you sell 3D prints on Etsy, chances are you spend more time than you'd like updating listings, checking which sale has just come in, copying customer details and working out whether you're actually making a margin on each piece. What starts out as the thrill of selling your designs ends up snowballing into a pile of repetitive admin.

To spare you that overload, the integration between Etsy and BolloPrint lets you bring both worlds together. When you connect your store, you don't just sync your catalogue of items — you also centralise incoming orders. That way your products, your customers and your sales stay organised, without constantly jumping from tab to tab or duplicating work by hand.

## Connect your Etsy store

- Go to **Settings → Extensions → Marketplaces** and find the Etsy card.
- Click **"Connect Etsy"**.
- Sign in with your store's seller account and authorise access to the platform.

When you come back to BolloPrint you'll see a green indicator confirming your store is ready to go.

## Sync your catalogue: publishing and importing

Once your store is connected, head to the **Marketplaces** section in the side menu to manage your products easily:

- **If you already sell on Etsy:** in the **Import** tab, pick your existing listings to bring them into BolloPrint with one click, descriptions, variants and photos included.
- **If you create products in BolloPrint:** in the **Publish** tab, turn your products and their variants into Etsy listings. You can leave them as drafts to review, or publish them straight away.

![The screen for editing an Etsy listing from BolloPrint: shipping, returns, category, tags, materials, and on the right the title, the description, the photos and the variants with their prices](/blog/media/2026-09-27-vender-en-etsy/02-edit-on-etsy.webp)

## Orders come in on their own

And once it's all synced… what do you do when you get a sale on Etsy? **Well, you don't have to do a thing!!**

When a customer buys on Etsy, the order lands on your BolloPrint **Production** board within seconds. You'll find the sale already created, with the customer's details, the products sold and the designs you need already linked, so you can get straight to printing!
