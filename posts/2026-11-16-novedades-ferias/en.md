Only a few weeks ago we told you what was new at the start of autumn, and we
haven't stopped since. This time the star is **selling**: online works as it always
has, but now you can also sell **in person**, at a 3D printing event, at a market or
at your workshop's counter. Here are the highlights.

## Let's start with the best part: what changes how you sell

### 1. Sell services and material too, not just what you print

Not everything you charge for comes out of the printer. **Products** now has a tab
for each thing you sell: **3D products · Services · Filaments · Resins ·
Supplies**.

| What | How it's sold |
|---|---|
| **Services** | Custom design, post-processing, assembly… whatever you offer, **by the hour or at a fixed price**. |
| **Filaments, resins and supplies** | The ones in your Inventory, with their price. When you sell them, **they come off your stock** automatically. |

Everything is added the same way to orders, quotes and the point of sale, and you
can tag it like any product.

![Products, Filaments tab: spools for sale with their price, margin and stock](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/02-productos-material-en.webp)

[See services](/help/servicios) · [See material for sale](/help/material-a-la-venta)

---

### 2. Point of sale: sell in person

A new add-on designed for maker markets, 3D printing events or your workshop's
counter. Sell straight from what you have in your inventory (3D prints, filaments,
supplies…).

- Add the products, apply a discount and take payment by **cash, card, Bizum or
  bank transfer**.
- Each sale is created as an **order that's already paid and delivered**.
- If you're paid in cash, enter how much you're given and **BolloPrint works out
  the change**.
- If you sell something that isn't in your catalogue, you can make a
  **"Custom sale"**.
- Use the **"Day summary"** to close the day.

![The point of sale with a sale in progress and a 15% discount](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/03-punto-de-venta-en.webp)

![Taking a cash payment: what you're given and the change to hand back](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/04-cobrar-en.webp)

:::warning
The point of sale **records** how you were paid, but it doesn't take the payment for
you: you still use your own card reader. Available on the **Pro** and **Max** plans.
:::

[See the point of sale](/help/punto-de-venta)

---

### 3. Events: prepare, sell at and close your markets

Before you set off, you prepare **what you're taking**: you take the pieces out of
your stock or print them for the occasion. During the event, the point of sale shows
you what you have to hand and what stayed at home: **"4 here · 5 at the workshop"**.

When you close the event you see **what it made you**: what you sold, your
expenses, your profit and what didn't come back. Whatever you didn't sell **goes
back to your stock automatically**.

![Closing an event: profit, sales, expenses, what doesn't come back, best sellers and what goes back to stock](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/05-cerrar-evento-en.webp)

:::info
Switch it on in **Settings → Extensions → Add-ons**. It goes hand in hand with the
point of sale: switching on Events switches on both. Available on the **Pro** and
**Max** plans.
[See events](/help/eventos)
:::

---

### 4. Price calculator

How much should I charge for this? Upload the slicer file (or type the details in
yourself) and the calculator adds up what it costs you and suggests a price using
your margins for each type of material. No separate spreadsheets.

When you're happy with it, from the same result you create a **quote**, a
**product** or a **design**.

![The calculator with a file uploaded: materials, unit cost, margin and final price](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/06-calculadora-en.webp)

:::info
It's an add-on on **every plan, Hobby included**.
[See the calculator](/help/calculadora)
:::

---

### 5. Add-ons: switch on only what you need

The point of sale, events and the calculator are **add-ons**: they don't come
switched on. You turn them on when you need them, and your menu stays tidy in the
meantime. If one day you stop using them, you switch them off and **nothing is
deleted**: when you switch them back on, everything is just as you left it.

There you'll also see the add-ons we're working on, under **"Coming soon"**. If one
interests you, press **"I'm interested"**: it helps us decide what to build first.

![Settings, Add-ons: point of sale, events and calculator switched on, and the upcoming ones with their "I'm interested" button](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/07-complementos-en.webp "70%")

:::info
You'll find them in **Settings → Extensions → Add-ons**. The Owner or an
Administrator switches them on.
[See add-ons](/help/complementos)
:::

### 6. Stock moves by itself with your orders

When you create, cancel or change an order or an event, BolloPrint shows you **what
comes out of your inventory** (pieces and material) and asks whether you take it from
your stock or print it. 3D products deduct their pieces. Orders that come in from
your online shops ask you when you open them.

![When creating an order: what comes out of stock, what needs printing, and whether to take it from stock or not](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/08-stock-pedido-en.webp "70%")

And **Inventory** has a new tab, **"History"**: see what changed, when, why and who
did it.

---

### 7. Your printer's SD card, from BolloPrint

On the printer's page, the **"Storage"** button shows you what the machine keeps on
its SD card, its USB stick or its internal memory: each print with its
**timelapse**, its **recording** and its **file**, plus a file browser to download
whatever you want. Nothing is deleted or changed on the printer. **Printers** also
has a **"History"** tab.

Today it works with **Bambu Lab** printers, printers running **Klipper**, the
**Snapmaker U1** and the **Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra 16K**, connected through the local
agent.

[See the printer's memory](/help/la-memoria-de-la-impresora)

---

### 8. Payments on the order and a Cash book

Each order records **how much has been collected, when and how**. It takes part
payments (a deposit, for example) and has a **"Collect the rest"** button. And if you
mark an invoice as paid, the payment is recorded on its order.

In Finance, the new **Cash book** shows what you collected each day and each month,
by payment method or by sales channel. It exports to **Excel or PDF**, ready for
your accountant. Available on the **Pro** and **Max** plans.

![The day's Cash book, with what was collected by payment method and each payment](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/09-diario-de-caja-en.webp)

---

### 9. A clearer Finance

- Every figure **includes tax and shipping**, just like your orders.
- **Transactions** has filters and totals, and you can look at **a single day**.
- **Sales** fits on one card: what you sell (3D products, services and material),
  your sales channels with their commission and your best customers.

![Finance, Sales tab: what you sell, how much each thing accounts for and its profit](/blog/media/2026-11-16-novedades-ferias/10-financiero-ventas-en.webp)

:::info
**Summary** and **Transactions** are available on every plan. **Sales**,
**Expenses**, **Suppliers**, **Taxes** and **Cash book** are available on the **Pro**
or **Max** plans.
[See Finance](/help/financiero)
:::
