Several orders arrived at once: fifteen spools of filament, four bottles of
resin, three brands, eight colours. Now they all need adding to the inventory,
one by one: find the brand, the material, the colour, the weight… And if you
don't do it there and then, a week later you no longer know how many spools of
black PETG you have left.

With "Add with a photo", all it takes is a few photos of the labels. In this
guide we show you how it works, step by step.

## First, what your inventory holds

In **Inventory**, BolloPrint keeps two things apart:

- **The Stock**: the **pieces you've already printed** and stored, ready to
  assemble into a product or sell.
- **The material**: your spools of **Filament**, your bottles of **Resin** and
  your **Supplies** (boxes, magnets, inserts…). It's what you buy, although you
  can sell it too!

"Add with a photo" only works for filaments and resins.

## Add with a photo, step by step

### 1. Take the photos

Go to your **Inventory**, open the **Filament** or **Resin** tab and tap
**Add with a photo**.

- **On a computer**, drag the photos onto the window or click it to choose
  them.
- **On a phone**, choose **Take photos** to use the camera or **From the
  gallery** if you've already taken them.

You can take **up to 50 photos in one go**: every spool that has just arrived,
in a single review. Try to get the label into each photo with as much
information as possible: name, barcode, serial number, brand… The more
information there is, the easier it is to identify.

![Two photos with the "Add with a photo" camera on a phone: the label on a box of Bambu Lab PLA Basic Gold filament and the label on a 1 kg bottle of Anycubic ABS-Like Clear resin, each one centred in the frame](/blog/media/2026-11-22-inventario-con-foto/03-camara-etiquetas-en-marco.webp "80%")

A tip: you can take the photos with your phone in the storeroom and review them
later on your computer. The review saves itself and you can carry on from
whichever device you like.

:::info 💡 Sharp photos first time
If a photo comes out blurry, BolloPrint tells you straight away so you can take
it again. There's a **torch** for the dark corners of the workshop. If you
prefer, you can open your phone's own camera app, take the photo and it goes
into the same pile.
:::

### 2. BolloPrint reads the labels

As you take photos, they upload and BolloPrint reads them one by one. There's no
need to wait with the window open: at the bottom you'll see "Uploading label
photos · 2 of 6" and you can keep working in another part of the app. If the
connection drops, the pending photos upload by themselves when it comes back.

![The review on a phone while BolloPrint keeps reading: sure materials, ones it isn't sure about and one requested for the catalogue with its case number, and at the bottom the Review (2) and Add 3 buttons](/blog/media/2026-11-22-inventario-con-foto/04-revisar-en-el-movil-en-marco.webp "40%")

### 3. Review only what needs it

When it's finished, BolloPrint shows you what it's sure about and what it
isn't. Now it's time to **Review the photos**, on your phone or later on your
computer. Each photo comes with a status.

![Review the photos on a computer: eleven materials read from the photos, with the All, Sure, To review and Done filters, one requested for the catalogue with its case number, the units of each one and the Review (5) and Add 5 buttons](/blog/media/2026-11-22-inventario-con-foto/05-revisar-las-fotos-caso-en.png)

With **Review** you only go through the doubtful ones; the sure ones stay in
the list. For the ones BolloPrint isn't sure about, you choose from the options
it suggests. And if none of them is right, **Search for another** lets you look
it up by hand in the catalogue. The search is forgiving: "PLA+ 2.0", "pla 2.0"
and "PLA+2.0" find the same thing, and you can type the colour in your own
language ("green", "orange"…).

![Which of these is it?: the photo with the filament box marked in red, what was read from the label and three possible colours of the same PLA to choose from, with their units](/blog/media/2026-11-22-inventario-con-foto/06-cual-de-estos-es-en.png "70%")

For each material you can see what was read from the label and how many units
you're about to add. If you've bought three identical spools, you adjust the
quantity with **+** and **−**, with no need to take three photos.

### 4. Add everything in one go

Nothing goes into your inventory until the end. Once you're done, tap **Add**
and every identified material is added at once. If any are still unidentified,
BolloPrint warns you first: you can go back and review them, or add the rest and
leave them out.

### What if your filament isn't in the catalogue?

It can happen with a small brand or a colour that's just come out. If
BolloPrint knows what it is but it isn't in the material catalogue yet, it lets
you know and automatically opens a case with our support team so they can add
it. **You don't have to do anything.** If it doesn't recognise it, you can
always ask us to add it straight from the photo.

:::info 🔒 Pro and Max plans
"Add with a photo" is included in the **Pro** and **Max** plans. The label
photos don't take up any of your plan's storage: they're deleted when the
review ends.
:::

Want all the details? What to do with each status, how the camera works or what
happens if the connection drops: it's all in the help for
[Add with a photo](/help/inventario#add-with-a-photo).

## Start with the next box that arrives

The next time a parcel of filament arrives, don't leave it for later: open
**Inventory**, tap **Add with a photo** and take the photos before you put the
spools away. In a couple of minutes your inventory is up to date.
